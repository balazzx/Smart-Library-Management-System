Important Data Management Rules
  • Always use *_Final.xlsx files — never old non-Final files
  • Roll Numbers preserve exact 3-digit suffixes (e.g., 2k24cse023)
  • ACCESS NO. (e.g., KIOT-CSE-0001) uniquely identifies physical copies
  • ISBN identifies book titles; ACCESS NO. identifies physical copies
  • Do NOT use Students.xlsx, Books.xlsx, or other old files
  • Do NOT generate fake students, books, or transactions
  • Do NOT require Staff_Librarians.xlsx (single librarian system)
  • Do NOT change valid Roll Number suffixes
